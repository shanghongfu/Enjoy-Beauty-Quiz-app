1. What is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Berlin
C. Paris
D. Madrid
Answer: C
Explanation: Paris has been the capital of France since the 10th century.

2. Which of the following are programming languages?
A. Python
B. HTML
C. JavaScript
D. CSS
Answer: A,C
Explanation: HTML and CSS are markup/styling languages, not programming languages.

3. The Earth is flat.
Answer: False
Explanation: The Earth is approximately an oblate spheroid.

4. Who wrote "Romeo and Juliet"?
A. Charles Dickens
B. William Shakespeare
C. Mark Twain
D. Jane Austen
Answer: B

5. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
A. Venus
B. Mars
C. Jupiter
D. Saturn
Answer: B
Explanation: Mars appears red due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

6. Which of the following are primary colors in the additive color model?
A. Red
B. Green
C. Blue
D. Yellow
Answer: A,B,C

7. The Great Wall of China is visible from space with the naked eye.
Answer: False
Explanation: This is a popular myth. The wall is not easily visible from space.

8. What does HTTP stand for?
A. HyperText Transfer Protocol
B. High Transfer Text Protocol
C. HyperText Transport Process
D. HyperText Transfer Process
Answer: A
